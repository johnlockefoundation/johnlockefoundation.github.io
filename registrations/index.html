Methodology

Figures are weekly snapshots of North Carolina voter registration from the Carolina Elections database (carolinaelections.com), one row per county per week since January 2008. “Other parties” combines the Green, Constitution, Libertarian, No Labels, Justice for All and We the People registrations. Racial categories are those published with the registration file: White, Black and Other.

The map is always the same fixed comparison: the change in the Democratic-minus-Republican share of each county’s registered voters between the latest weekly snapshot and the snapshot four weeks earlier, expressed in percentage points. The date selectors in the county card and the table change only those panels, never the map. Registration is not a vote, and shifts in registration do not by themselves forecast an election.
